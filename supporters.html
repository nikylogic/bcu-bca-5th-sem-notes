Stealthify

Real-Time, Invisible AI Copilot for Virtual Meetings & Technical Interviews

Stealthify is the premier meeting copilot engineered with zero-bot technology. Unlike traditional tools that join calls as intrusive recording bots, Stealthify runs natively on your desktop to deliver ultra-low latency conversational assistance, smart cues, and real-time guidance during live virtual sessions.

🛡️ Display Shield™ Invisibility
Complete screen-share stealth. Floating cues, answers, and overlays remain 100% invisible to interviewers and meeting participants during Zoom, Microsoft Teams, and Google Meet screen shares.
⚡ Zero-Bot Architecture
Operates natively as a discreet local overlay without inserting an uninvited, recorded third-party bot into the meeting room roster.
🎯 Real-Time Conversational Cues
Processes audio with sub-second latency to generate precise talking points, technical interview solutions, and objection-handling guidance live.

🏆 #1 in Dictation & Voice-to-Prompt Community Supporter

iVibe

Open-Source AI Voice Dictation & Voice-to-Prompt Engine for Developers

iVibe (ivibe.tech) is the next-generation desktop application for Windows, macOS, and Linux that revolutionizes voice input. Tailored specifically for engineers, computer science students, and power users, it streamlines workflows with AI coding agents like Cursor, Claude Code, and terminal environments.

🎙️ System-Wide Instant Dictation
AI-driven voice-to-text that automatically cleans up stutters, filler words ("um", "uh"), and false starts, pasting polished, structured text wherever your cursor is placed.
💡 Voice-to-Prompt Transformation
Speak your thoughts naturally without worrying about phrasing. iVibe packages your stream-of-consciousness ideas into crisp, high-context prompts for LLMs and AI chats.
🛠️ Voice + Visual Bug Fix for Coding Agents
Combines voice explanations with automatic screen capture and visual markup (powered by Meta SAM), complete with local redaction of passwords and API keys before submission.
Visit Official Website: ivibe.tech
